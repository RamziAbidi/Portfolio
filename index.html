About

I'm a PLC programmer engineer at Mondragon Assembly, where I configure, commission and support automated assembly machines on three continents. Before that I managed electrical and automation maintenance at a ceramics plant. I recently finished a master's thesis on a ROS 2 defence module for the NOMAD rover. I'm drawn to robotics, embedded systems, mechanical design and industrial computing.

Projects

Master thesis, AVIONAV · Mar – Dec 2025

SAMURAI: automated defence module for the NOMAD rover

Integrated the rover into ROS 2 and built its robotic architecture, with navigation and control. Fused LiDAR, an RGB-D camera, an infrared camera and an IMU for perception and mapping, and added computer vision and AI for automated threat detection. Tested and validated everything in simulation.

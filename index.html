About

I'm a robotics and automation engineer with more than six years in industry. For the last four, I've programmed Beckhoff PLCs and commissioned automated machines built around SCARA and 6-axis robots (ABB, ESTUN) and vision cameras, working on site for clients in India, Spain, Algeria and Morocco.

I'm still learning, and I hold two master's degrees: the first in electromechanical engineering, the second in Advanced Robotic Systems Engineering and Artificial Intelligence. My thesis was a ROS 2 rover that combines LiDAR, RGB-D, infrared and IMU sensors with AI-based threat detection. I want to keep bringing robotics and AI research into industrial automation.
